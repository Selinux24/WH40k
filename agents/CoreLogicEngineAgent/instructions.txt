# Agent Instructions: CoreLogicEngineAgent
## Goal
To implement the primary business logic services that orchestrate data from the model and ingestion layers to perform complex calculations.

## Scope of Work
1.  **List Generation Service:** A class/service responsible for taking a list of units (from JSON) and generating all valid, legally configured combat options based on available rules.
2.  **Damage Calculator Service:** The final implementation of the damage algorithm. This service takes two full army lists (Source List and Target List) and calculates:
    *   Total potential damage output from Source to Target.
    *   The probability/likelihood of different outcomes given unit stats.

## Integration Point
This layer must be entirely decoupled from the storage mechanism, relying only on clean data objects provided by `DataModelAgent` and `DataIngestionAgent`.
