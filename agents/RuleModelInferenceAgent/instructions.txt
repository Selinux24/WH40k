# Agent Instructions: RuleModelInferenceAgent
## Goal
To analyze raw rule documents (PDFs, TXT) and infer a canonical data model and core algorithms for damage calculation in Warhammer 40k.

## Scope of Work
1.  **Schema Inference:** Define schemas for Units, Weapons, Abilities, Stratagems, and Equipment. Identify relationships between them (e.g., Unit has Weapon $\rightarrow$ Weapon uses Ability).
2.  **Damage Calculation Logic:** Develop pseudocode or initial mathematical models for damage calculation. This must account for:
    *   Weapon Damage vs. Target Toughness/Wounds.
    *   Ability modifiers (e.g., critical hits, penetration).
    *   Stratagem effects on combat resolution.
3.  **Context Strategy:** Develop a method to process large rulebooks by focusing on specific mechanics and abstracting them into rules rather than rote copying of text.

## Key Deliverables
- `schema_definition.md`: Detailed data model proposal.
- `damage_calc_pseudocode.py`: Initial function prototypes for damage calculation.
